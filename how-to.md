# How to Use WithYou  
*A complete guide to how the app works — and how the pieces fit together*

WithYou is designed for moments when starting, staying focused, or getting back on track feels hard.  
It is **not** a task manager you need to keep perfectly organized.

You can use one feature or all of them. There is no wrong way.

## Today View (Your home base)

**Today** is the main screen of the app.  
It’s the place you open when you want the app to answer, *“What matters right now?”*

Today adapts to your state and your day instead of forcing a plan.

### Sections in Today

### Right now
This section answers one question:

> “Is there anything I need to deal with immediately?”

- If there *is* something urgent or relevant, it will appear here.
- If there isn’t, you’ll see a calm confirmation like:
  > *“You’re clear for now.”*

This is intentional.  
Being “clear” is information — it tells you you’re not behind.

**What to do here**
- If something appears: you can act on it or jump straight into a Focus Session.
- If nothing appears: you don’t need to invent work.

### If you have energy
This section is optional by design.

It answers:
> “If I *do* feel okay and want to do more, what would be a good next thing?”

- It only shows something if there’s genuinely something useful.
- If it says *“Nothing extra needed today,”* that’s permission to stop.

This prevents the ADHD pattern of:
> finishing one thing → immediately feeling pressure to do *everything else*

### Reset
This section is for moments when your nervous system needs help — not more tasks.

You’ll see:
- **Refocus (30 seconds)** – a short reset to pause and re-center
- **I’m stuck** – a signal that you’re blocked and need gentler guidance

These are not failures.  
They’re tools for moments when pushing won’t work.

### Completed today
This shows anything you finished today — including Focus Sessions.

Each item includes the phrase:
> *“That counted.”*

This is deliberate reinforcement:
- Progress is real, even if it felt small
- Starting counts
- Stopping counts

Today is as much about *acknowledgment* as it is about action.

## Focus Sessions (Protecting your attention)

A **Focus Session** is a short window where the app helps you stay with **one thing**.

It’s not about productivity.
It’s about reducing the friction of starting and returning.

### Creating a Focus Session

When you tap **Focus**, you’ll see:

#### Focus on…
This is where you name the thing you’re working on.
Examples:
- “Do some stuff”
- “Read chapter 3”
- “Open the document”

Vague is okay. This is for *you*, not reporting.

#### Optional start step
This is one of the most important features.

You can write the *smallest possible first action*, such as:
- “Open the first app I’ll use”
- “Write one sentence”
- “Just start”

This helps bypass task paralysis.

#### Duration
You choose how long you want the container to last:
- 25 min
- 45 min
- 60 min
- even 1 minute

Short sessions are valid.  
The goal is *entry*, not endurance.

### During a Focus Session

Once it starts, the screen changes to reinforce one idea:

> **“Only this matters right now.”**

You’ll see:
- A countdown timer
- The task name
- Simple actions:
  - **Pause**
  - **Refocus**
  - **Add thought**
  - **End**

#### Add thought
If something pops into your head:
- Tap **Add thought**
- Park it
- Return to focus

You don’t need to remember it. The app remembers for you.

#### Refocus
If you notice you’ve drifted:
- Tap **Refocus**
- Pause briefly
- Gently return

Refocus is not punishment.  
It’s a reset.

### When time ends (or you stop)

When the timer ends, you’ll see options:
- **Extend 5 minutes**
- **Keep going**
- **Wrap up**

Nothing auto-forces you.

If you choose to end, the app asks:
- Did you complete it?
  - **Yes, completed** → moves to *Completed Today* and clears it from Inbox/Schedule
  - **No, just stopping** → no penalty, no shame

This protects you from “I didn’t finish so it doesn’t count.”

## Brain Dump / Capture (Getting thoughts out of your head)

**Capture** is where you put things you don’t want to hold in your mind.

You’ll see language like:
> *“What’s pulling at your attention? Park it here so you can focus.”*

This is not a to-do list.
It’s mental offloading.

### When to use Capture
- A thought interrupts a Focus Session
- You remember something unrelated
- Your brain feels crowded
- You’re anxious about forgetting

### How it works
- Type or dictate a thought
- Save it
- You’re done

You don’t need to organize it yet.

## Inbox (Your holding area)

The **Inbox** is where captured or unscheduled items live.

Think of it as:
> “Things that exist, but not *now*.”

Inbox items often include:
- A task name
- An optional suggested start step
- An estimated small duration

### Inbox Item actions

When you open an Inbox item, you can:

- **Schedule**  
  Give it a specific time so it appears in Schedule and Today.

- **Make smaller**  
  Break it into something more approachable (e.g. 2–5 minutes).

- **Mark completed**  
  If it’s already done.

- **Not needed**  
  Explicitly let it go. This is a feature, not a failure.

Inbox gives you control without forcing decisions upfront.

## Schedule (Time-based commitments)

**Schedule** is for things that must happen at a specific time.

Examples:
- “Do some things at 11:30pm”
- Appointments
- Time-bound tasks

Each scheduled item can include:
- A start instruction
- A small first step
- A time anchor

Schedule feeds **Today**, so:
- Upcoming items surface automatically
- Missed items can be gently checked in on later

## Refocus (Outside Focus Sessions too)

You can use **Refocus** even when you’re not in a Focus Session.

Refocus is for moments like:
- You feel stuck
- You’re overwhelmed
- You’ve been avoiding something
- You’re looping mentally

It’s a 30-second reset — not a lecture.

# Putting it all together: Real scenarios

## Scenario 1: “I feel overwhelmed and don’t know where to start”
1. Open **Today**
2. See *“You’re clear for now”* — okay, no urgency
3. Tap **Capture**
4. Dump everything on your mind (30–60 seconds)
5. Return to **Today**
6. Choose something small from Inbox or “If you have energy”
7. Start a **Focus Session** with a short duration
8. Use **Add thought** or **Refocus** if needed

## Scenario 2: “I have time and energy right now”
1. Open **Today**
2. Look at **If you have energy**
3. Choose the suggested next thing
4. Tap into **Focus** directly
5. Work until time ends
6. Mark completed or stop — either counts

## Scenario 3: “I keep getting distracted while working”
1. Start a **Focus Session**
2. When distracted:
   - Tap **Add thought** to park it
   - Or tap **Refocus**
3. Return gently
4. If you stop early, that’s okay — end the session cleanly

## Scenario 4: “I missed something and feel bad about it”
1. Open **Today**
2. Notice anything surfaced as missed
3. Decide:
   - Still important → Schedule or Focus
   - Not important → Let it go
4. No backlog guilt required

## Scenario 5: “My brain is loud but I don’t want to work yet”
1. Open **Today**
2. Tap **Refocus (30 seconds)** or **I’m stuck**
3. Breathe
4. If clarity comes, proceed
5. If not, stop — the app still did its job

## The core idea

- **Capture** frees your mind  
- **Inbox** holds things without pressure  
- **Schedule** anchors time  
- **Today** tells you what matters  
- **Focus** protects attention  
- **Refocus** helps you return  
- **Completed Today** reminds you that it counted  

With You isn’t about doing more.

It’s about making *starting, continuing, and stopping* gentler.
