Moments

Help for the hard little moments.

Small, quiet tools for when starting, focusing or remembering feels heavier than it should.

  • Refocus

    A one-minute breathing reset with your own mantra, from anywhere in the app.

  • I’m stuck

    One tiny next step. Too big? Make it smaller. Two minutes is enough.

  • Still relevant?

    Missed something? WithYou asks once, kindly, then lets it go.

  • Make it smaller

    On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, WithYou suggests an even smaller first step, entirely on device.

  • Reminders that help you start

    Each reminder says how to begin, not just what you forgot.

  • Siri & Shortcuts

    Capture a thought, start a focus session, or ask for help without opening the app.

What WithYou won’t do

  • No scores.

  • No red badges.
  • No nagging.

Privacy

Your thoughts stay on your phone.

Everything you capture lives on your iPhone. No account, no ads, no analytics. AI help runs on your iPhone when it can. Cloud AI is off unless you turn it on, and WithYou never stores your words.

FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

Is it free?

Yes, during the beta. The core relief features (Capture, Inbox, Focus sessions, Refocus and I’m stuck) will always be free. No ads, ever.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your thoughts, tasks and focus sessions stay on your iPhone. There’s no account and no analytics. Cloud AI is off by default. If you turn it on, only what a request needs is sent, and WithYou never stores your words.

What do I need?

An iPhone with iOS 17.6 or later. You’ll get access through TestFlight.

What if I fall behind?

Nothing in WithYou is ever overdue. Missed reminders are asked about once, then let go. Come back whenever you’re ready.

Is this medical advice?

No. WithYou is a support tool, not a medical device or treatment. If you’re in crisis, contact local emergency services or a trusted support line.
